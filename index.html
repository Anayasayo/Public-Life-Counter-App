Life Counter

Free Magic: The Gathering life counter. Works offline, with no account or ads.

iPhone & iPad

Apple only allows App Store apps to install with one tap. This app is free and off the store, so it installs through SideStore, a free app that signs it with your own Apple ID. You set it up once on a computer (about 15 minutes). After that, installs and updates happen on the phone.

One-time setup

  1. On the iPhone, install LocalDevVPN from the App Store, open it and allow the VPN.
  2. On a Mac or Windows PC, download iloader from the SideStore setup guide.
  3. Plug the iPhone in with a cable, open iloader, sign in with any Apple ID, pick your phone and choose Install SideStore (Stable).
  4. On the iPhone: Settings → General → VPN & Device Management, tap your Apple ID under Developer App and tap Trust.
  5. Settings → Privacy & Security → Developer Mode: turn it on. The phone restarts.
  6. Connect LocalDevVPN, open SideStore and sign in with the same Apple ID.

Stuck? The full SideStore install guide has screenshots for each iOS version.

Install the Life Counter

  1. Copy this link:
  2. In SideStore, open Sources, tap +, paste the link and add it.
  3. Find 3DPG Life Counter and tap Free. It lands on your home screen.

Keeping it working

Apps signed with a free Apple ID last 7 days. SideStore renews it in the background when LocalDevVPN is on and you're on Wi-Fi. You can also open SideStore → My Apps and tap the days counter. Your games and settings are kept. Updates show up in SideStore too.

Android & Fire tablets

Download the app
  1. Tap Download the app on the phone or tablet.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If asked, allow your browser to install unknown apps (Fire tablets: Settings → Security & Privacy → Apps from Unknown Sources).
  3. Tap Install. To update, download and install the new version over the old one. Your games are kept.
